HOW IT WORKS

One intake flow. Clear decisions.

Designed around the exact lead-processing use case used in the portfolio project.

01

Capture & validate

Collect contact details and one unstructured business request, then handle only basic transport, contact and anti-spam checks before AI qualification.

02

AI qualification

Determine whether the request contains enough evidence to qualify, then extract requirement, product, budget, timing, pain points and the next sales action as structured JSON.

03

Score & classify

Use the AI-extracted signals to calculate a repeatable priority score from budget, timing, service fit and buying intent.

04

Route & log

Upsert the result to the CRM, alert sales only when the workflow determines that the opportunity is high priority, and keep an audit log.
